# Navigation rules

The rules of how a person moves through Songmaker, each under the ID its test cites.

## Navigation

- **1354_rule_1** In the full player, a tap anywhere on the line jumps the song there. *e.g. You tap at three quarters of a 3:00 song and it plays from about 2:15. Not: Only dragging the small knob moves it.*
- **1354_rule_2** While you drag, the knob grows and the time you will land on shows above your finger; the song jumps when you let go. The desktop bar shows the same time above the pointer. *e.g. You drag to 1:12, see '1:12', and it plays from there. Not: The sound stutters while you drag.*
- **1354_rule_3** 'Played' means your own listening, the same in Listen, in the Last played sort and in the desktop list. Creating or editing an album does not count. *e.g. You create album 'Test' and Listen does not change. Not: A new album that was never played jumps to the top.*
- **1354_rule_4** A song you hear inside a playlist puts the playlist into Listen, not the song's album. *e.g. You play Late Drive and it shows 'stopped at song 5'. Not: Cold Harbour moves up as well.*
- **1354_rule_05** Write counts your own work on a song: a new song, saved lyrics or style, a rename, a new take, a co-writer message. Listening does not count. *e.g. 'Quiet Fire · lyrics edited · 10:01'. Not: A song you only listened to shows up in Write.*
- **1354_rule_06** Listen and Write show only what you did; someone else playing or editing your songs never moves them. *e.g. A second account plays your album and your Home stays the same. Not: Another person's work lifts your album.*
- **1354_rule_7** Library shows only albums and Playlists only playlists, as covers on the desktop. The mixed wall and the 'Added' sort go. *e.g. 'Library · 12' shows 12 albums. Not: A playlist among the albums.*
- **1354_rule_8** One switch, A–Z or Last played, sorts Library, Playlists and the desktop list the same way, and this device remembers it. *e.g. You choose Last played, the left list follows, and it is still Last played tomorrow. Not: The left list stays A–Z.*
- **1354_rule_9** An empty Library or Playlists says so in one line and points to + NEW. The tab never disappears. *e.g. 'No playlists yet.' Not: A blank page.*
- **1354_rule_10** On the phone, Library is a list: cover, name, song count and ▶ on each row. ▶ plays the album without opening it, and the playing album is marked. *e.g. You tap ▶ on Echo Valley, the row turns purple and you stay in the list. Not: ▶ opens the album.*
- **1354_rule_11** An album with nothing to hear yet has no ▶. *e.g. A new album with 0 takes shows no ▶. Not: A 'No takes' error after the tap.*
- **1354_rule_12** Back from an album returns to the list exactly where you left it, and that album lights up for a moment. *e.g. You open Lunar Static from the bottom of 43 albums, tap ‹, land at the bottom again and Lunar Static flashes. Not: Landing 155 px short, as today.*
- **1354_rule_13** With 43 or more albums or many playlists, every item stays on one row: long names end in '…' and the song count stays visible. *e.g. 'The Extraordinarily Lo… 8 songs'. Not: Rows that wrap.*
- **1354_rule_14** Home is two lists of 3 rows in the Library row look: Listen (albums and playlists you played) and Write (songs you worked on). Each thing appears at most once; + NEW stays on Home; Continue goes, with its fold. *e.g. Two songs from Cold Harbour give one row. Not: A 'Continue ▾' fold.*
- **1354_rule_15** In Listen, ▶ continues where you stopped, the playing row shows pause, and a tap on the row opens it. *e.g. You tap ▶ on Late Drive and song 5 plays. Not: It starts again at song 1.*
- **1354_rule_16** A tap on a Write row opens that song in the song editor. *e.g. You tap Quiet Fire and the editor opens. Not: It starts playing.*
- **1354_rule_17** Starting another album never loses your place in the first. *e.g. You were on song 3 of Cold Harbour, play Fever Garden, then tap ▶ on Cold Harbour in Listen: song 3 plays. Not: Cold Harbour starts from the top.*
- **1354_rule_18** When nothing has been played or written yet, each list shows one line saying what will fill it. *e.g. 'Albums you play show up here.' Not: An empty heading.*
- **1354_rule_19** Offline, Home keeps the lists it last showed; a Home that never loaded says so and offers Retry. On a new device, Listen and Write are the same as on your other devices. *e.g. In airplane mode the same six rows stay. Not: The lists vanish.*
- **1354_rule_20** Deleting or archiving an album, playlist or song takes it off Listen and Write at once, and the delete's Undo brings it back. *e.g. You delete Quiet Fire and Write shows the next song. Not: A row that opens 'No such song'.*
- **1354_rule_22** A tap on a song plays it: you stay on the album, its box turns purple, and the pencil ✎ opens the song editor. *e.g. You tap Quiet Wind and it plays; ✎ opens Edit · Co-writer · Takes. Not: The tap opens the editor.*
- **1354_rule_23** A tap on a song that has no take yet opens the song editor. *e.g. You tap a new song and the editor opens. Not: An error, or a tap that does nothing.*
- **1354_rule_24** The breadcrumb and the shuffle button leave the album and playlist page at both widths. Shuffle lives in the player. *e.g. The album page shows cover, title, Play, ⋯ and the songs. Not: 'Library › Broken Lanterns' above the title.*
- **1354_rule_25** Every phone page you opened from another one has ‹ at the top left, named after where it goes back to; with nothing before it, it goes to that kind's list. *e.g. From Listen you open Cold Harbour, and '‹ Home' takes you back to Home. Not: Settings with only the phone's own Back.*
- **1354_rule_26** 🔍 sits in the top bar on every phone page and finds albums, songs and playlists. *e.g. 'lantern' finds Broken Lanterns. Not: Search disappears with the drawer.*
- **1354_rule_27** The account circle keeps Settings, the theme switch and Log out. After Log out, the next person on this phone sees none of your Home, player card or last song. *e.g. Your partner signs in after you: their Home, their card. Not: Your Listen on their screen.*
- **1354_rule_28** On the phone, Home, Library and Playlists are tabs at the bottom, with the same names, icons and order as the desktop sidebar. The ☰ drawer goes. *e.g. Three tabs, always visible. Not: ☰ and tabs at the same time.*
- **1354_rule_29** Each tab keeps its place: switch away and back and you find the same page at the same scroll. Tapping the tab you are on returns to its list. *e.g. Lunar Static open in Library, switch to Home and back: Lunar Static is still open. Not: Library starts at the top again.*
- **1354_rule_30** The phone's Back first closes whatever is open, then goes back one page within the tab; on the Library or Playlists list it goes to Home, on Home it leaves the app. Switching tabs never adds to Back. *e.g. Album → Back → Library list → Back → Home → Back → out of the app. Not: Back walks through every tab you touched.*
- **1354_rule_31** A reload keeps you on the same tab and page. Home is /, Library /library, Playlists /playlists; album and playlist addresses stay. *e.g. You reload on /library and stay there. Not: A reload drops you on Home.*
- **1354_rule_32** On the song editor the tabs step aside. ‹ brings you back with the tabs; with unsaved changes it asks first, exactly as Back does. With the keyboard open, tabs and card step aside. *e.g. You edit lyrics, tap ‹, and are asked before the draft is dropped. Not: A draft lost on ‹.*
- **1354_rule_33** Settings shows the tabs and the player card, with no tab lit. *e.g. You open Settings and still see the three tabs. Not: Settings without a way to Home but Back.*
- **1354_rule_34** On the phone the player is a card above the tabs: cover, song, album, play/pause, ⏭ and a progress line, with a handle on top. A tap or swipe up anywhere else on the card opens the full player. *e.g. You tap the song name in the card and the full player opens. Not: Only an unmarked part of the bar opens it.*
- **1354_rule_35** ⏮, shuffle and everything else live in the full player, which closes with ⌄ at the top left or by pulling down. *e.g. Shuffle is in the full player. Not: Shuffle on the card.*
- **1354_rule_36** On a device where nothing was played yet, the card shows the top Listen entry, paused where you stopped, and ▶ plays it. With nothing playable at all, there is no card. *e.g. New phone: the card shows Cold Harbour, song 3. Not: 'No takes (Picks)' after ▶.*
- **1354_rule_37** The open album or playlist is marked clearly in the left list, with a bar and a bold name, much stronger than hover. *e.g. Lighthouse Code open: its row has a purple bar and bold text. Not: A mark weaker than the mouse hover, as today.*
- **1354_rule_38** The open album is always scrolled into view in the left list: after opening it from Home, a cover, search, Back or a reload. *e.g. You open Lunar Static from the covers and the list shows its row. Not: Its row far below the visible part, as today.*
- **1354_rule_39** On the desktop, every page you reached from another one has ‹ left of its title, doing what the browser's Back does. *e.g. From the covers you open Glass Rivers, tap ‹, and are back at the covers in the same place. Not: Only the browser's Back.*
- **1354_rule_40** The desktop sidebar starts with Home above Library and Playlists; the app name also leads Home. A click on the word Library opens the album covers; the arrow next to it folds the list open or shut. The same for Playlists. *e.g. Click 'Library': all album covers. Click ›: the list folds open. Not: An extra 'All albums' row you have to find.*
- **1354_rule_41** The album name in the desktop player bar opens the playing song's album. *e.g. Click 'Cold Harbour' in the bar and the album opens. Not: No way from the bar to the album, as today.*

## Album page

- **album_rule_1** A new song lives at the end of the album’s track list: a quiet “+ New song” row turns into the inline “New song in `<album>`” card (title, CREATE, ×). An empty album says “No songs yet.” above that row. It is the only way to make a song; the mixed Create form goes.
- **album_rule_2** The header actions are Play · Shuffle · ⋯ only, on their own row.
- **album_rule_3** ⋯ opens the one popover form at both widths, clamped 8 px inside the screen, 7 rows: Edit details · Cover · Pick album takes · Add to playlist · Share · Archive · Delete (red, last). Rename folds into Edit details, the three cover rows into one Cover row.
- **album_rule_4** The cover is edited in place: no standing cover card. A tap on the cover (the “Add cover” placeholder when there is none) grows it where it stands; suggestions come one image at a time, ‹ › or a swipe browse those made so far, “n / m”, one row Upload · Suggest another · USE · ×.
- **album_rule_5** Suggest runs through the agent service. When a suggestion cannot run, the editor names one of five problems, busy, not enabled, unavailable, log in again or a spent budget.
- **album_rule_6** A suggestion keeps running when you leave: Back, Escape, another page or another album stop nothing and discard nothing; only ×, USE and Upload stop a running suggestion and discard the unused ones.
- **album_rule_7** Delete and Archive keep their confirmation and rights; nothing here changes who may do what.
- **album_rule_8** The grown cover keeps its place and shows a turning ring and the time since the server started the run, Making a cover · 0:42; no percentage, because the image call has none. The time comes from the job’s start, so it is right after coming back and on a second device.
- **album_rule_9** The cover carries a quiet strip at its foot: a small turning ring and the time, 0:58. It counts every unused suggestion, from the server, the same on every device (“● 3 waiting”). The mark stays until USE, Upload or × discards the unused ones; opening and closing the editor does not remove it.
- **album_rule_10** Rows carry no play glyph — the row is the target, the playing row shows the equalizer.

## Create, account and settings

- **account_rule_1** One + NEW, on Home’s title line at both widths, with or without albums. It opens the one popover form — Album · Playlist; choosing one unfolds its inline card (album: Title, Artist optional, CREATE, ×; playlist: Name, CREATE, ×). On the phone it is the only create entry for either; on the desktop the Library and Playlists heads carry it too.
- **account_rule_2** Phone: the account is the circle top right on the three tab pages, beside 🔍. A page opened from another one shows ‹ in the brand’s place and no circle; a song page’s bar belongs to the song and carries no circle.
- **account_rule_3** On the phone /settings is the Settings list, with the tabs and the player card and no tab lit (1354_rule_33).
- **account_rule_4** Wortlaut: „Log in“ und „Log out“, im Menü und überall.
- **account_rule_5** Desktop: Kreis mit Menü oben rechts, wie am Telefon; keine Konto-Zeile mehr in der Leiste.
- **account_rule_6** Cleanup ist der letzte Tab der Admin-Seite.
- **account_rule_7** Rechtliches: Legal notice · Privacy · Terms of use als stille Zeile unten im Konto-Menü, je in neuem Tab auf hallucinai.de (Ziele: Impressum, Datenschutz, Nutzungsbedingungen); so bleibt das Impressum von jeder angemeldeten Seite erreichbar.
- **account_rule_8** An empty list says one line: “No albums yet.” An empty Library or Playlists says so and points to + NEW; the tab never disappears.
- **account_rule_9** The desktop rail keeps the Settings group.
- **account_rule_10** The account menu is the one popover form: Settings, the Light/Dark theme switch, Log out last.
