At the Take

The idea: the song page has two columns that each own one moment — Write (recipe, style, lyrics as one growing document) and Listen (the takes of the current version, earlier versions folded) — and everything that changes an existing take happens at that take: ⋯ → Repaint or Cover opens a task card right under the take row, with its waveform, its range or its sliders and its own button, and × as the only way out (points 3 and 7). The core loop stays: write, Generate in the header or the phone bar, the new take appears at the top of the Listen column (point 2). The recipe is closed by default: one quiet line, “Recipe ›”, with no tabs and no values, because nothing changed is nothing to read (operator, 22.10.2026). Opened, it shows the tabs Song · Generate · Engine with their fields — the tab names carry no values — and ˄ closes it again. The one thing the closed line ever shows is a dot, “Recipe •”, when anything differs from its standard (default or preset); opened, the dot sits on the tab and on each changed field, each changed field has a ↺, and each tab one “Reset all”. The Engine tab keeps the expert knobs out of the way (points 5 and 7). There is no “From a take” tab: Repaint and Cover are tasks on a take, not settings of the recipe. A run is one or the other, never both, and the page shows one task card at a time. The seed taken from a take is not in the recipe either: ⋯ → Use seed puts a chip right at the Generate button, “Seed · take 11 ×”, visible exactly while it takes effect — the next Generate, a Repaint or a Cover — and × removes it; the task card says the same in one quiet line, “seed: from take 11”. Style and lyrics are boxes that grow with their text and the page scrolls — on both devices — because a scroll box inside a page is what looks cut off (point 4). The co-writer is the second tab of the Listen column on desktop and the middle tab on the phone (point 6). The take ⋯ menu stays as it is today, plus one row, Use seed.

What goes away, what moves versus today (origin/main 09dbb05c)

Goes away

  • The chip strip above the page — the recipe is one closed line, “Recipe ›”, that shows no values; a dot is the only thing it ever adds.
  • Values in the tab names — the open strip says Song · Generate · Engine and nothing else; the fields are under the tab.
  • The permanent “Repaint: Off · conservative · balanced · aggressive” row and the second “Source: … Repaint | Cover ×” bar — the task card at the take is the only Repaint/Cover, and only one card exists at a time.
  • “Seed Random | Pinned” and “Pinned 0” — the seed is a chip at the Generate button, set only by a take’s Use seed, cleared by ×, never a recipe value.
  • The ghost SAVE beside the style prompt (already ruled into the song ⋯ as “Save version”).
  • The fixed boxes with their own scrollbars for style and lyrics.
  • Ten archived takes at full height on the song page — folded into their versions.
  • The Sound · Text · Reproduce panel spanning both columns.

Moves

  • Model, Takes → the Generate tab; BPM, Duration, Key, Voice, Reference → the Song tab; Preset, DiT/LM fields, negative prompt, thinking switches → the Engine tab. The seed → the chip at the Generate button, and the task card’s quiet “seed: from take 11”.
  • “Changes mark the draft” → the dot: on the closed line, on the tab, on the field; ↺ resets one field, “Reset all” one tab.
  • Repaint’s range picker and modes, Cover’s two strengths → the task card under the take, whose button names the verb and the range.
  • Now Playing’s row reads “Use seed” (D1, #1816); the take ⋯ menu gains “Use seed” with the seed as its hint — the menu is otherwise today’s.
  • The Co-writer toggle in the header → second tab of the Listen column.
  • Earlier versions → one folded row each; a row opens its takes (archived rows compact) and “Open vN” loads it as draft, as blessed.
  • Phone: the “Recipe › XL-TURBO · …” summary row of the blessed picture → the same closed line as on desktop, “Recipe ›” without values, opening into the same three tabs.
Scroll decision. Desktop: the page scrolls as one and Takes scrolls with it; the Co-writer pane is bounded to the viewport so its message list scrolls inside it and the composer stays on screen. Phone: the page scrolls, boxes grow, keyboard up hides the bars; a scroll box would fight the keyboard for the few lines left. Where a task lives: at the take, under its row, one open at a time; opening another task — on this take or another — closes the first. The page’s Generate stays what it is — a new take from the recipe — because the card has its own button that says what it does; two different verbs, two different places, no dimming.
