The current rail is a plain tree

The rail keeps the live GitLab-like hierarchy: compact uppercase group rows, one indented tree, the active red edge and the account at the bottom. Search is one independent field under the brand. Large covers belong to Library’s wall, never to the rail.

Decision: The rail keeps its current look: a tree with cover icons, one search field at the top, one collapse control. Browsing covers is the wall. Library is the home: a Continue row of up to six places — albums and playlists, each once, newest work first, each naming the song you were on — then the wall. Playlists carry the same tile as albums: a 2x2 mosaic of their albums’ covers unless an uploaded cover replaces it.

(a) Song · rail open

Desktop

The tree is the one album list. Every group row — Library, Playlists and Settings — opens or closes across its whole width; its caret only shows that state. “All albums · 42” is the first child and opens the Library wall. An album opens its songs; a song opens the editor.

(d) Library · cover wall

Desktop

Library is the browsing destination. Its group is closed in the rail while the Continue row keeps the last places close — one tile per album or playlist, never a song tile, never a place twice, at most six, archived albums left out. Under the place stands the song you were on, under that when; a tap opens exactly that song on the tab you left. Listening, lyric edits, generating takes and co-writer turns all move a place up. Then large album and playlist covers form the wall, in the order the albums page’s sort switch holds (A–Z by default; picture owner album-browsing.html (g)); Continue is never re-sorted by it.

(g) Rules

Interaction
One Takes list per screen.

Desktop has the Takes column. On phone, Write has the Takes chips. Now Playing identifies the playing take; it never becomes a second list.

One row, one target.

A group row opens or closes across its whole width; its caret is display only. Children navigate: “All albums · 42” to the wall, albums to albums, songs to songs, pages to pages.
