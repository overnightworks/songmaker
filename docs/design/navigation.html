The rules (operator, ruled 15.10.2026, #1354)

Numbered as in #1354, so a slice can cite “#1354 rule 25”. Rule 21 is ruled later and is not part of this picture.

S1 · Seek

  1. In the full player, a tap anywhere on the line jumps the song there. e.g. You tap at three quarters of a 3:00 song and it plays from about 2:15. Not: Only dragging the small knob moves it.
  2. While you drag, the knob grows and the time you will land on shows above your finger; the song jumps when you let go. The desktop bar shows the same time above the pointer. e.g. You drag to 1:12, see '1:12', and it plays from there. Not: The sound stutters while you drag.

S2 · Data

  1. 'Played' means your own listening, the same in Listen, in the Last played sort and in the desktop list. Creating or editing an album does not count. e.g. You create album 'Test' and Listen does not change. Not: A new album that was never played jumps to the top.
  2. A song you hear inside a playlist puts the playlist into Listen, not the song's album. e.g. You play Late Drive and it shows 'stopped at song 5'. Not: Cold Harbour moves up as well.
  3. Write counts your own work on a song: a new song, saved lyrics or style, a rename, a new take, a co-writer message. Listening does not count. e.g. 'Quiet Fire · lyrics edited · 10:01'. Not: A song you only listened to shows up in Write.
  4. Listen and Write show only what you did; someone else playing or editing your songs never moves them. e.g. A second account plays your album and your Home stays the same. Not: Another person's work lifts your album.

S3 · Library

  1. Library shows only albums and Playlists only playlists, as covers on the desktop. The mixed wall and the 'Added' sort go. e.g. 'Library · 12' shows 12 albums. Not: A playlist among the albums.
  2. One switch, A–Z or Last played, sorts Library, Playlists and the desktop list the same way, and this device remembers it. e.g. You choose Last played, the left list follows, and it is still Last played tomorrow. Not: The left list stays A–Z.
  3. An empty Library or Playlists says so in one line and points to + NEW. The tab never disappears. e.g. 'No playlists yet.' Not: A blank page.

S4 · Phone list

  1. On the phone, Library is a list: cover, name, song count and ▶ on each row. ▶ plays the album without opening it, and the playing album is marked. e.g. You tap ▶ on Echo Valley, the row turns purple and you stay in the list. Not: ▶ opens the album.
  2. An album with nothing to hear yet has no ▶. e.g. A new album with 0 takes shows no ▶. Not: A 'No takes' error after the tap.
  3. Back from an album returns to the list exactly where you left it, and that album lights up for a moment. e.g. You open Lunar Static from the bottom of 43 albums, tap ‹, land at the bottom again and Lunar Static flashes. Not: Landing 155 px short, as today.
  4. With 43 or more albums or many playlists, every item stays on one row: long names end in '…' and the song count stays visible. e.g. 'The Extraordinarily Lo… 8 songs'. Not: Rows that wrap.

S5 · Home

  1. Home is two lists of 3 rows in the Library row look: Listen (albums and playlists you played) and Write (songs you worked on). Each thing appears at most once; + NEW stays on Home; Continue goes, with its fold. e.g. Two songs from Cold Harbour give one row. Not: A 'Continue ▾' fold.
  2. In Listen, ▶ continues where you stopped, the playing row shows pause, and a tap on the row opens it. e.g. You tap ▶ on Late Drive and song 5 plays. Not: It starts again at song 1.
  3. A tap on a Write row opens that song in the song editor. e.g. You tap Quiet Fire and the editor opens. Not: It starts playing.
  4. Starting another album never loses your place in the first. e.g. You were on song 3 of Cold Harbour, play Fever Garden, then tap ▶ on Cold Harbour in Listen: song 3 plays. Not: Cold Harbour starts from the top.
  5. When nothing has been played or written yet, each list shows one line saying what will fill it. e.g. 'Albums you play show up here.' Not: An empty heading.
  6. Offline, Home keeps the lists it last showed; a Home that never loaded says so and offers Retry. On a new device, Listen and Write are the same as on your other devices. e.g. In airplane mode the same six rows stay. Not: The lists vanish.
  7. Deleting or archiving an album, playlist or song takes it off Listen and Write at once, and the delete's Undo brings it back. deletion · spoken yes e.g. You delete Quiet Fire and Write shows the next song. Not: A row that opens 'No such song'.
  8. While a song's take is generating, its Write row says 'generating'. later e.g. 'Neon Call · generating'. Not: Nothing shows that a take is on its way.

S6 · Album page

  1. A tap on a song plays it: you stay on the album, its box turns purple, and the pencil ✎ opens the song editor. e.g. You tap Quiet Wind and it plays; ✎ opens Edit · Co-writer · Takes. Not: The tap opens the editor.
  2. A tap on a song that has no take yet opens the song editor. e.g. You tap a new song and the editor opens. Not: An error, or a tap that does nothing.
  3. The breadcrumb and the shuffle button leave the album and playlist page at both widths. Shuffle lives in the player. e.g. The album page shows cover, title, Play, ⋯ and the songs. Not: 'Library › Broken Lanterns' above the title.

S7 · Phone top bar

  1. Every phone page you opened from another one has ‹ at the top left, named after where it goes back to; with nothing before it, it goes to that kind's list. e.g. From Listen you open Cold Harbour, and '‹ Home' takes you back to Home. Not: Settings with only the phone's own Back.
  2. 🔍 sits in the top bar on every phone page and finds albums, songs and playlists. e.g. 'lantern' finds Broken Lanterns. Not: Search disappears with the drawer.
  3. The account circle keeps Settings, the theme switch and Log out. After Log out, the next person on this phone sees none of your Home, player card or last song. rights · spoken yes e.g. Your partner signs in after you: their Home, their card. Not: Your Listen on their screen.

S8 · Phone tabs

  1. On the phone, Home, Library and Playlists are tabs at the bottom, with the same names, icons and order as the desktop sidebar. The ☰ drawer goes. e.g. Three tabs, always visible. Not: ☰ and tabs at the same time.
  2. Each tab keeps its place: switch away and back and you find the same page at the same scroll. Tapping the tab you are on returns to its list. e.g. Lunar Static open in Library, switch to Home and back: Lunar Static is still open. Not: Library starts at the top again.
  3. The phone's Back first closes whatever is open, then goes back one page within the tab; on the Library or Playlists list it goes to Home, on Home it leaves the app. Switching tabs never adds to Back. e.g. Album → Back → Library list → Back → Home → Back → out of the app. Not: Back walks through every tab you touched.
  4. A reload keeps you on the same tab and page. Home is /, Library /library, Playlists /playlists; album and playlist addresses stay. e.g. You reload on /library and stay there. Not: A reload drops you on Home.
  5. On the song editor the tabs step aside. ‹ brings you back with the tabs; with unsaved changes it asks first, exactly as Back does. With the keyboard open, tabs and card step aside. e.g. You edit lyrics, tap ‹, and are asked before the draft is dropped. Not: A draft lost on ‹.
  6. Settings shows the tabs and the player card, with no tab lit. e.g. You open Settings and still see the three tabs. Not: Settings without a way to Home but Back.

S9 · Player card

  1. On the phone the player is a card above the tabs: cover, song, album, play/pause, ⏭ and a progress line, with a handle on top. A tap or swipe up anywhere else on the card opens the full player. e.g. You tap the song name in the card and the full player opens. Not: Only an unmarked part of the bar opens it.
  2. ⏮, shuffle and everything else live in the full player, which closes with ⌄ at the top left or by pulling down. e.g. Shuffle is in the full player. Not: Shuffle on the card.
  3. On a device where nothing was played yet, the card shows the top Listen entry, paused where you stopped, and ▶ plays it. With nothing playable at all, there is no card. e.g. New phone: the card shows Cold Harbour, song 3. Not: 'No takes (Picks)' after ▶.

S10 · Desktop list

  1. The open album or playlist is marked clearly in the left list, with a bar and a bold name, much stronger than hover. e.g. Lighthouse Code open: its row has a purple bar and bold text. Not: A mark weaker than the mouse hover, as today.
  2. The open album is always scrolled into view in the left list: after opening it from Home, a cover, search, Back or a reload. e.g. You open Lunar Static from the covers and the list shows its row. Not: Its row far below the visible part, as today.
  3. On the desktop, every page you reached from another one has ‹ left of its title, doing what the browser's Back does. e.g. From the covers you open Glass Rivers, tap ‹, and are back at the covers in the same place. Not: Only the browser's Back.
  4. The desktop sidebar starts with Home above Library and Playlists; the app name also leads Home. A click on the word Library opens the album covers; the arrow next to it folds the list open or shut. The same for Playlists. e.g. Click 'Library': all album covers. Click ›: the list folds open. Not: An extra 'All albums' row you have to find.
  5. The album name in the desktop player bar opens the playing song's album. e.g. Click 'Cold Harbour' in the bar and the album opens. Not: No way from the bar to the album, as today.
