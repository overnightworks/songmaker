Every shared page is the player

Left: today, in real screenshots of shared links opened signed out on a phone (test album "Glass Rivers"). Right: the same moment after the change. Under each pair is the exact list of changes.

Blessed 15.10.2026, frozen 16.10.2026 (#1432). The before screenshots are dropped; the blessed artifact keeps them. The after frames, change lists and the order are unchanged. [Frozen copy: the 'Left' screenshots the lede names are not in this file; read each section as the after frame with its change list.]

One rule for every shared link: the page is the player for what you shared. At the top it is always the same: the big cover, the title, a big ▶ and the slider. It is the app's own full player, in the same order everywhere: cover, title, controls and slider; then the lyrics of the song now playing, which follow along; then, for an album or a playlist, its songs. A shared song simply has no song list. Albums and playlists add shuffle and ⏮ ⏭. Nothing is hidden behind a second player, and there is no second place for lyrics.
